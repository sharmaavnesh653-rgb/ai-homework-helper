    Split the velocity into components

  2. 2

    Use the equation with no time in it

Our commitments

Straight answers

Is this cheating? +

The product is built so that it is not. Hint mode gives one nudge and refuses to produce the answer even if you push. Check mode reviews your own work and will not rewrite it. Walkthrough mode shows the reasoning and then hands you a question it deliberately leaves unanswered. You still do the thinking.

What can I give it? +

Typed or pasted text, a photo of a page, or a PDF worksheet — up to four files at once. Maths and science problems, essay paragraphs, reading-comprehension questions, or your own half-finished attempt.

Will it just make things up? +

It is instructed to say plainly when it is not sure rather than asserting a confident wrong answer, and never to claim your work is correct without checking it. It is still AI: verify the reasoning before you hand anything in.

Where is my saved work stored? +

On your device, in your browser. Saved notes and history never leave it, which also means clearing your browser data clears them.

Can my teacher see what I did? +

Yes, and that is deliberate. A session reads as a tutoring transcript — the question, the reasoning, the check question. Nothing is designed to be hidden from a teacher.
